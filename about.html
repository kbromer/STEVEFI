About Steve-FI

Steve-FI (Smart Tools for Economic Vitality and Empowerment through Financial Inclusion) is an AI-powered financial inclusion assistant designed to help individuals navigate financial challenges with empathy, expertise, and practical guidance.

Our virtual agent, Ash, is trained on comprehensive financial inclusion resources and best practices developed by financial security experts. Ash provides personalized support to help you understand your financial situation, identify opportunities, and take meaningful steps toward financial stability.

What Steve-FI Can Help With

  • Understanding your financial situation — Review income, expenses, and identify areas of concern
  • Document analysis — Upload and analyze bank statements, bills, pay stubs, and budgets
  • Cost-saving opportunities — Identify ways to reduce expenses and optimize spending
  • Assistance programs — Find eligible government and community support programs
  • Local resources — Connect you to financial services, credit counseling, and support organizations
  • Action plans — Receive clear, practical next steps tailored to your situation

How It Works

Steve-FI uses OpenAI's Realtime API to provide live voice conversations with our AI agent, Ash. Simply click "Connect with an Agent," allow microphone access, and start talking. You can also upload financial documents (PDFs or images) at any time during the conversation for detailed analysis.

All conversations are private and secure. Your audio and documents are processed in real-time and are not stored permanently.

Training Resources

Ash is trained on the following financial inclusion resources and best practices:

  • ASP Financial Inclusion System Report (February 2024)
  • ASP Financial Security Worker Executive Summary (May 2022)
  • FDIC Scenarios for Financial Inclusion
  • Money matters: A toolkit for caseworkers to support adult and adolescent clients with basic money management

Important Notes

  • Steve-FI does not provide legal, medical, or mental health advice
  • We do not collect or store sensitive personal information like full SSNs or bank account numbers
  • Recommendations are educational and informational — always verify with qualified professionals
